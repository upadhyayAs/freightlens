WHY THIS MATTERS

Shipment conditions can change faster than planning cycles.

Weather, operational disruption, holidays and geopolitical conditions can affect the same shipment at the same time. The challenge is bringing the relevant signals together and deciding when human intervention is actually needed.

🌐
Fragmented risk signals
Critical shipment information can sit across weather, calendars, operational updates and external event sources.
⚡
Conditions change quickly
A route that looked acceptable at booking can become more exposed as conditions change during the shipment lifecycle.
🧑‍💼
Humans handle exceptions
Low-risk shipments can follow an automated path while higher-risk scenarios are escalated to a decision-maker.
The principle: automate the assessment, explain the risk, and keep the human in the decision loop when the exposure warrants it.
